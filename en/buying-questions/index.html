Volume, weight and quotations

How do I total cubic metres for cartons of different sizes?

Group cartons by size and multiply each carton’s outer length, width and height in metres, then multiply by the number of cartons. Ten 0.60 × 0.40 × 0.40 m boxes plus five 0.50 × 0.40 × 0.30 m boxes total 1.26 m³. Verify packed dimensions and measure any palletized load separately.

Mixed-carton volume guide · Answer link

How do I compare air freight quotes that use dimensional weight?

Give both forwarders the same carton list and gross weights. Ask for the divisor, whether the formula applies per carton or per shipment, how weights are rounded and any minimum charge. Calculate chargeable weight for each quote and compare pickup, transfer and final delivery on the same scope.

Mixed-carton volume guide · Answer link

Can I quote loose-carton volume once the cartons have been palletized?

Keep both records. After palletization, measure the widest points of the wrapped load, including pallet height, and record gross weight and stackability. Ask the forwarder which data set is used for pricing and which is required at pickup. The final charge may depend on the form actually tendered.

Mixed-carton volume guide · Answer link

How do I tell whether two delivery-inclusive quotes cover the same charges?

List pickup, export handling, linehaul, destination handling, clearance support, taxes and last-mile delivery separately. Mark each line as fixed, estimated or excluded. Use the same shipment and address. Ask for written terms for appointments, liftgate or unloading if the receiving location requires them.

Mixed-carton volume guide · Answer link

What must I recheck if the supplier changes the pack from 80 cartons to 72?

Confirm the product count, then obtain quantities, external dimensions and gross weights for each new carton size. Ask the forwarder to reconfirm total volume, chargeable weight, loading requirements and existing quote validity. Retain both packing-list revisions so pickup uses the correct data.

Cutoffs, handoffs and timing

Can I schedule a Friday pickup when the supplier says the goods will be ready on Friday?

Confirm that ready means sealed cartons, marks, a packing list, pickup location and an available loading window. Obtain confirmation for the driver or receiving warehouse. Record production readiness and physical handover as separate events, and schedule around the last outstanding dependency.

Cargo-ready and cutoff guide · Answer link

How can an operations team avoid mixing up the document cutoff and warehouse cutoff?

Put document submission, warehouse receipt and booking confirmation in separate fields with date, time zone, owner and confirmation method. Ask the forwarder which missed cutoff will affect the booking. Agree on an escalation point and update the same schedule whenever a milestone changes.

Cargo-ready and cutoff guide · Answer link

What should I record after a consolidation warehouse says it received the goods?

Record physical receipt time, warehouse or recipient, carton count, visible damage and handoff reference. Confirm the message refers to physical receipt. If counts or condition differ, preserve photographs and the source packing list and reconcile them before using the figures downstream.

Cargo-ready and cutoff guide · Answer link

How do I work backward from a fixed international delivery date?

Start with the receiving address, appointment window and who unloads. Work back through last-mile delivery, destination handling, linehaul and the origin cutoff. Obtain feasible dates and conditions from each party. Keep estimates and contingency time visible until the relevant bookings are confirmed.

Cargo-ready and cutoff guide · Answer link

How do I decide whether to wait for several suppliers or split a shipment?

Track warehouse receipt, carton counts and expected completion by supplier. Ask for storage, booking change and split-shipment costs. Compare waiting and partial shipment against the final delivery deadline. Once the buyer confirms the decision, update each packing list and handover record.

Routes and delivery terms

How should I compare air, sea and rail quotations for the same shipment?

Use the same origin, destination, goods, service scope and cargo-ready date. Request the milestones and charge breakdown, with schedule and space conditions. Check cargo acceptance and the receiving deadline before comparing total cost and items that could change.

Routes and markets · Answer link

Does a door-delivery quote include import taxes, clearance and unloading?

Give the full receiving address and ask the forwarder to specify the trade basis and responsibility for import and export formalities, taxes, delivery and unloading. Add any appointment or equipment needs as separate lines. Compare coverage based on the written scope agreed by both sides.

Routes and markets · Answer link

How can I get a useful freight quote if supplier pickup addresses are not yet confirmed?

Share the cities or likely districts, full cargo breakdown and expected readiness date. Ask for separate options for supplier drop-off and collection from multiple premises, with their assumptions written down. Confirm the collection price and window once the actual loading addresses are known.

Routes and markets · Answer link

How should I include a warehouse appointment in an international delivery plan?

Document the address, booking method, warehouse reference, time window, vehicle limits and delivery record required. Confirm who reserves the slot, when the reference will be shared and what applies to changes. Keep the appointment confirmation and proof of delivery as separate milestones.

Routes and markets · Answer link

Can I use my outbound freight quote for a return from the same country?

Reconfirm the return origin, recipient, handover window, cargo condition and packed dimensions. Ask the forwarder and the party responsible for customs to verify the return documentation and scope. Request separate charges for collection, storage, repacking and return transport where needed.

Cargo records and exceptions

What information should a first shipping brief contain beyond the carton count?

Provide origin, full destination, cargo description, count, dimensions and gross weight by external carton type, expected readiness and required arrival. Disclose batteries, liquids or special handling needs and ask what documents the carrier requires. Have the quote record these inputs.

Fugia service information · Answer link

How do I trace one missing carton during consolidation?

Reconcile the pickup record, warehouse photos and receipt count by supplier, handover reference and carton marks. Compare outer cartons with outer cartons. Establish the last confirmed handoff, log the person responsible and next update, then reconcile totals before a replacement or document change.

Fugia service information · Answer link

What evidence should a receiver retain when cartons are damaged but none are missing?

Follow the delivery procedure for recording visible damage and photograph the load, carton marks and damaged areas. Record the time, reference and affected carton count and retain packaging. Notify the agreed party and document any unpacking inspection separately from the exterior condition.

Which records need an update after measured packing dimensions change?

Issue a dated packing revision with carton count, outer dimensions and gross weights. Ask the responsible parties to confirm changes to freight, warehouse requirements and booking details, and recheck marks before pickup. Mark the old version obsolete so receiving and collection use the current record.

How can several suppliers label cartons to avoid mixing them at a consolidation warehouse?

Use a clear shipment reference and carton-number sequence per supplier, with purchase reference and counts in the same packing-list format. Assign a distinct number to each partial shipment. Review photographs before pickup and reconcile the count by those identifiers at the warehouse.
