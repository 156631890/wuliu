SHIPPING FAQ

Clear answers before you ship.

What should I check if a Maersk booking has not materialised after the first vessel ETD?

Maersk's notice of 15 October 2026 took effect that day: eligible bookings that have not materialised may be automatically cancelled when five days or more have passed since the first vessel's estimated time of departure (ETD). It does not say every booking is cancelled on day five. Prepare the booking reference, first-vessel ETD and current status; confirm with the actual booking party or Maersk Customer Service before coordinating supplier arrivals and consolidation. These are shipment-checking suggestions, not Maersk's official definition of a materialised shipment.

Source: Maersk advisory (15 October 2026)

What freight services does Fugia provide from China?

Fugia provides FCL and LCL ocean freight, air freight, China-Europe rail and multimodal transport, door-to-door shipping, customs support, and warehousing and consolidation in Yiwu. Channel availability must be confirmed for the cargo, destination and delivery requirements.

What information is needed for a freight quote?

Provide the cargo description, package count and dimensions, gross weight and total volume, battery status, origin, destination country and city, and preferred shipping and arrival dates. Specify consolidation, export clearance, import clearance and final-delivery needs so the transport method and quote scope can be assessed.

Can goods from several Chinese suppliers be consolidated?

You can discuss multi-supplier consolidation in Yiwu, including sorting, labeling and palletization. Before suppliers dispatch goods, confirm the cargo list, warehouse arrival plan, receiving address and handling requirements, then consolidate and ship under the agreed plan.

Does door-to-door shipping always include duties and customs fees?

No. Door-to-door describes the transport arrangement; duties, taxes and customs services depend on the agreed trade terms and itemized quote. Before shipping, confirm the exporter, importer, party responsible for taxes and the documents required at destination.

How are transit times confirmed?

Transit time depends on the transport method, origin and destination, capacity, customs and final delivery. Route ranges on this site are planning references, not arrival guarantees. Share your delivery deadline and confirm when the quoted transit time starts and which transport stages it covers.
